Is The Innkeeper Worth It?

The Innkeeper is absolutely worth reading if you enjoy the 'misunderstanding' trope and kingdom-building. It starts slow, with a protagonist who feels like a puppet on system-enforced life support, but it evolves into a massive, multi-realm epic.

My score: 8.5/10.

What Is The Innkeeper?

The story follows Lex, an ordinary man who becomes the owner of the Midnight Inn — a mysterious establishment outside space and time. Bound to a snarky system, Lex must fulfill quests to upgrade the Inn and host guests from across the multiverse.

This is a classic "fake it till you make it" scenario on a cosmic scale, perfect for readers who like seeing a "weak" MC outsmart the universe.

My Review

I've spent a significant amount of time with Lex and his Inn, and as of Chapter 1005, my opinion has shifted significantly from where I started.

The Early Struggle: Puppet or Protagonist?

In the first 100 chapters, Lex is frustratingly ordinary. He often feels like a puppet being moved by the system's whims.
