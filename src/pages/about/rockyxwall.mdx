## who even is rockyxwall?

okay so. at some point i hit a wall. i had read so many webnovels, so many manga chapters, watched so much anime
that i genuinely ran out of stuff that matched my taste. not like "i can't find anything right now" — i mean i
actually exhausted the genres i liked. that's not a brag, it's just what happens when you consume stories the
way i do.

i'm not a 30-minutes-today, 30-minutes-tomorrow type of person. when i'm into something i sit down and i go.
full day. if a manga has finished its story in novel form, i'm reading the novel. i don't rewatch. i don't reread. 
i just keep moving forward until there's nothing left.

so when you've consumed that much, two things happen. one — you develop opinions. strong ones. you know what
actually works in a story and what's just filler dressed up in pretty art. two — you start wanting to tell
other people what's worth their time so they don't have to waste it on the stuff that isn't.

that's what this site is. i'm not a critic, i'm not a journalist, i'm just someone who's been deep in this
world long enough to have a real read on it. i won't sugarcoat. if something is mid i'll say it's mid. if
something actually wrecked me in the best way i'll tell you that too.

if you're also someone who ran out of good things to read and you're desperately hunting for the next story
that won't disappoint you — you're in the right place. welcome.
