## who even is lazynoman?

okay so. at some point i hit a wall. i had read so many webnovels, so many manga chapters, watched so much anime
that i genuinely ran out of stuff that matched my taste. not like "i can't find anything right now" — i mean i
actually exhausted the genres i liked. that's not a brag, it's just what happens when you consume stories the
way i do.

i'm not a 30-minutes-today, 30-minutes-tomorrow type of person. when i'm into something i sit down and i go.
full day. if a manga has finished its story in novel form, i'm reading the novel. i don't rewatch. i don't reread. 
i just keep moving forward until there's nothing left.
