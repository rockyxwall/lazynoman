## What is Above Myriads?

**Above Myriads** (also widely known as *Beyond Myriad Peoples* and *Above Ten Thousand People*) is a Chinese **manhua** — not a Korean manhwa — published by **Boyi Animation (博易动漫)**. It is a manhua adaptation of a popular Chinese web novel, and it has built a massive following across multiple reading platforms.

The story follows **Yi Feng**, a man transported into a cultivation world who has absolutely no spiritual roots and cannot cultivate in the traditional sense. Yet everything he does is accidentally extraordinary — the pills he makes offhandedly are divine-grade, the techniques he casually demonstrates become lost legends, and the world's most powerful cultivators grovel at his feet while he remains genuinely convinced he is just an ordinary mortal.

> Tens of thousands of big shots grovel at his feet, yet he says: *"I'm really just an ordinary mortal, though…"*

It's a comedy-cultivation story at heart, and one of the best in the genre if you enjoy misunderstandings played to perfection.

## Does It Have a Source Novel?

**Yes — Above Myriads is a manhua adaptation of a Chinese web novel.**

The source novel is:

> **I Am Unaware That I Am the Peerless Martial God**
> Chinese title: **玄幻：原来我是绝世武神**
> Author: **风凌北 (Fēng Líng Běi)**

The novel currently has **1,377+ chapters** and is still ongoing. It shares the exact same premise and protagonist as the manhua — Yi Feng (also romanized as Ye Feng in some translations), a man transported to a cultivation world who can't cultivate but accidentally becomes the most terrifying existence in that world through his mortal-world skills and knowledge.

### Novel Summary

Ye Feng was transported to a cultivation world, but unfortunately had no hope of cultivating immortality and could only live as an ordinary mortal. He only wished to live a peaceful life.

Yet he didn't know —

- The dog he adopted, because it ate his food, became a legendary demon king.
- The kitchen knife he casually forged became a peerless divine weapon, causing numerous experts to fight desperately for it.
- The young disciple he took in became an immortal lord that led an era, due to cultivating the techniques Ye Feng taught him.
- Even the skeletons he looked down on the most were ominous demon kings that suppressed countless worlds.

Many years later, looking at the void shattered with a punch, Ye Feng murmured to himself: *"It turns out the peerless kings were myself all along…"*

## Which Novel Chapter to Start at After the Manhua

This is the question most fans ask once they've caught up with the manhua translations. Because the manhua condenses and sometimes reorders events from the novel, there's no perfect 1-to-1 chapter match. Here's the general guidance from fans who have read both:

- If you've read up to around **manhua chapter 100**, you've roughly covered **150–200 novel chapters** worth of story.
- By **manhua chapter 200+**, the manhua slows its pacing relative to the novel, so you may have only covered around **250–300 novel chapters** of content.
- The **safest approach is to start the novel from Chapter 1.** The novel adds significant side character development, world-building details, and jokes that the manhua cuts for pacing. Re-reading familiar events in novel form doesn't feel repetitive because the delivery is different.
- If you want to skip the overlap, start around **novel Chapter 200–250** and read backward if something feels out of context.

> The community consensus on NovelUpdates is that **the manhua executes the comedy better**, while the novel has more story depth and is the only way to read content beyond where the manhua is currently adapted.

## Novel vs. Manhua — Which Is Better? {#novel-vs-manhua}

Both are worth your time, and most fans end up reading both.

**Read the manhua if you:**
- Want the best version of the comedy — visual gags and Chinese internet meme references land much better in manhua form
- Prefer a faster, more polished reading experience
- Are new to the story and want to get hooked before committing to 1,000+ novel chapters

**Read the novel if you:**
- Have caught up with the manhua and can't wait for new chapters
- Want more story depth, more side characters, and more world-building
- Want to read content the manhua hasn't adapted yet (the novel is far ahead)

The general fan opinion: the **manhua does the comedy better**, and the **novel does the story better**. They complement each other well, and neither replaces the other.

## Quick FAQ

### Is Above Myriads a manhwa or manhua?

It is a **manhua** — Chinese, not Korean. The confusion is very common because many readers find it on the same platforms used for Korean manhwa, and the art style can feel similar.

### What is the name of the novel that Above Myriads is based on?

The source novel is **"I Am Unaware That I Am the Peerless Martial God"** (玄幻：原来我是绝世武神), written by **风凌北**. It has 1,377+ chapters and is still ongoing.

### Are the novel and manhua the same story?

Yes — same protagonist, same cultivation world, same core misunderstanding comedy. The manhua adapts the novel, with some scenes condensed, reordered, or expanded for the format.

### Who created the manhua?

The *Above Myriads* manhua was created by **Boyi Animation (博易动漫)** and is officially published through **Bilibili Comics**.

### How many chapters does the novel have?

As of early 2026, the novel has **1,377+ chapters** in Chinese and approximately **1,348 chapters** translated into English, with new chapters releasing regularly.

### How many chapters does the manhua have?

The manhua is ongoing with over **300 chapters** translated into English as of early 2026.

### Why does the manhua have so many different titles?

The Chinese title 万人之上 has been translated differently by each scanlation group. Because fan translations existed before the official English release, several names took hold across different platforms. They all refer to the same story.

### Where can I read both for free?

- **Manhua:** Mgeko, MangaDex, or the official Bilibili release on Bato.To
- **Novel:** NovelFire, NovelBin, or FreeWebNovel — check NovelUpdates for the best current translation source
