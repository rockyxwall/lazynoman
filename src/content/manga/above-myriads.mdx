## Is Above Myriads Worth It?

*Above Myriads* is **absolutely worth reading** if you enjoy the 'misunderstanding' trope taken to its comedic extreme. It is one of the funniest cultivation manhua currently available, perfectly balancing high-stakes action (that the MC ignores) with absurd slapstick. While the plot formula is repetitive, the execution of the "mortal god" gag is so polished that it remains fresh even after hundreds of chapters. My score: **8.5/10**.

## What Is Above Myriads?

*Above Myriads* is a Chinese manhua (also known as *Beyond Myriad Peoples*) that parodies the "invincible protagonist" genre. The story follows Yi Feng, a man who believes he is a failed transmigrator with no talent for cultivation. In reality, he is so powerful that he exists outside the world's laws, but he interprets every miracle he performs as a stroke of luck or a mundane occurrence.

The world is populated by terrified "big shots" (Sect Leaders, Immortal Emperors) who view Yi Feng as an unfathomable master, while he views them as friendly neighbors or customers for his "ordinary" shop. The tone is light, fast-paced, and heavily focused on the comedy of errors.

## My Review
I've followed this series for over 300 chapters, and it has consistently been my go-to "feel good" read. It doesn't try to be deep; it tries to be funny, and it succeeds where most "OP MC" stories fail by leaning into the absurdity of the situation.

### What It Does Right

The art quality is surprisingly high and stays consistent, which is rare for long-running manhua. The facial expressions—especially the "terrified master" vs. "clueless MC" contrasts—are gold. The way the author builds up a massive threat only for Yi Feng to accidentally sneeze it out of existence is a formula that, surprisingly, doesn't get old. It's a masterclass in the "One Punch Man" style of tension management.

### Where It Struggles

The supporting cast can sometimes feel one-dimensional. Once a character enters Yi Feng's orbit, they usually stay in a permanent state of shock and groveling. Additionally, the plot progression is slow; because the MC is unaware of his status, he doesn't have a "goal" beyond living a quiet life, which can make the world-ending threats feel a bit like background noise.

## Read If…

- You love **One Punch Man** or **The Eminence in Shadow**.
- You want a **stress-free** read where the MC is never in danger.
- You enjoy **high-quality manhua art** and clean layouts.
- You like seeing arrogant cultivators get humbled accidentally.

## Skip If…

- You hate the **misunderstanding trope** (it's the whole show).
- You want a **serious plot** with high stakes and MC growth.
- You prefer **complex, multi-layered side characters**.

## What Others Think

The community is generally obsessed with this series. It's often cited as the "best version" of the clueless OP MC trope, surpassing rivals like *I'm Really Not the Demon God's Lackey* in terms of pure comedy. Most readers praise the art and the comedic timing, though some veterans of the genre note that it starts to feel formulaic after the 200-chapter mark.

The naming confusion is the biggest point of discussion; fans often have to explain that *Above Myriads*, *Beyond Myriad Peoples*, and *Above Ten Thousand People* are all the same story.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Above Myriads worth reading in 2026?
Yes! It remains a top-tier comedy manhua. If you haven't started it yet, you have a massive backlog of high-quality chapters to binge.

### How many chapters does Above Myriads have? Is it finished?
It is ongoing with over 400 chapters in the original Chinese release. English translations are usually close behind. It shows no signs of ending soon as the world keeps expanding.

### Does Above Myriads have a novel?
Yes. The manhua is based on the web novel *I Am Unaware That I Am the Peerless Martial God* (我真不是盖世高人). The novel is much further ahead and provides more context on Yi Feng's origin.

### What should I read after Above Myriads?
If you liked the comedy, try *The Strongest System* or *The Ultimate Scheming System*. For more clueless MC energy, *I'm Really Not the Demon God's Lackey* is a must-read.
