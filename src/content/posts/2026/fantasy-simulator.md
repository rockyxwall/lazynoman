## What to Expect

The novel is easy to describe: there are no important characters except the MC (Chen Heng). The story is a pure world-hopper where he jumps from one world to another with different power systems, eventually becoming the strongest being in the multiverse. Side characters are introduced and forgotten as soon as their arc ends. The MC is extremely cold and calculating, focusing purely on his own gain. Whether that's the entire point of the novel or not, the story keeps you engaged because you always want to see what he does next. The pacing is very fast at the beginning, though it slows down over time. The world-building isn't particularly deep across the multiverse—each world feels disconnected with little continuity between them. Overall, it's a solid read if you want a fast-paced world-hopping novel with a ruthless, benefit-driven MC.

### Worlds & Realms

* **Modern Reality (Earth):** The starting modern martial arts world; later discovered to be a collapsing dreamscape. *(Linling High School, Martial Society)*
* **Kailin / Nardo Realm (Knight World):** Medieval feudal realm of knights, noble politics, and royal academy training. *(Kutu Principality, Nardo Kingdom)*
* **Sorcerer World (Hemu Region):** High-magic world of rune meditation, spell constructs, and wizard academies. *(Hunter's House, Dawn Empire)*
* **Azure Heaven Realm (Xianxia World):** Classic Daoist immortal cultivation realm with sects and lightning tribulations. *(Daoist Sects, Prince Liunan's Court)*
* **World of Gods (Karo / Hatim):** Western fantasy realm ruled by ancient churches, divine domains, and the Goddess's Magic Net. *(Hatim Kingdom, Dragon Island)*
* **Star Alliance (Sci-Fi World):** Advanced space-faring civilization blending psychic beastmastery with galaxy mechas. *(Hollow World, Hechi Star)*
* **Primogenitor World (Violet Empire):** Ancient dynastic empire governed by divine bloodlines inherited from progenitor gods. *(Violet Empire)*

### Power Systems

* **Martial Arts (Body Forging):** Refines body and organs via Qi Blood. Stages: Body Forging Completion *(Solid Rock Body)* → Martial Body → Martial Illumination. *(World: Modern Reality)*
* **Life Knights:** Ignites a heart Life Seed into Life Armor and wings (1st–3rd Ring). *Drains lifespan when active.* *(World: Knight World)*
* **Sorcery & Runes:** Visualizes mental Rune Seeds (1st–9th Ring) and absorbs beast blood via Warlock bloodline refining. *(World: Sorcerer World)*
* **Immortal Cultivation:** Absorbs ambient Spirit Qi. Stages: Qi Refining → Foundation Building → Spirit Transforming → Nascent Soul → Deity Transformation → Immortal God. *(World: Azure Heaven Realm)*
* **Faith & Divinity:** Condenses mortal worship into divine authority (e.g., Slaughter Divinity) and accesses the Goddess's Magic Net. *(World: World of Gods)*
* **Beastmastery & Mechas:** Uses Telekinesis Seeds to bond with Imperial Beasts; high-tier practitioners pilot willpower-amplified Galaxy Mechas. *(World: Star Alliance)*
* **Bloodline Primogenitors:** Power inherited from primordial progenitor gods (Sun, Silver Moon); locked until seized via the Trial of Origins. *(World: Primogenitor World)*

I started this novel on 12/05/2024 and finished it on 06/06/2026, so it took me over two years to finish (though not in one continuous run).

I originally read up to chapter 390 and dropped it because I was craving a pure cultivation setting, whereas the story kept pivoting to Western fantasy and faith-based worlds. Later, when I had nothing else to read, I picked it back up and powered through the rest in a few days.

The beginning (first ~390 chapters) was great—I'd easily rate that first half **3.9 / 5.0**. But the second half felt forced, repetitive with its world-conquering loops, and the ending was unsatisfying because it felt like the prologue to a brand-new arc rather than a true conclusion. Overall, my final rating sits at **2.9 / 5.0**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Fantasy Simulator complete or ongoing?
It is completely finished in both raw Chinese and English translations. The main story concludes at Chapter 846, followed by several backstory extra chapters ending at Chapter 853.

### Does Fantasy Simulator have romance or a harem?
No meaningful romance. The MC (Chen Heng) occasionally marries or takes partners during various simulations for political stability or bloodline optimization, but he treats all relationships purely transactionally.

### How does the simulation system work?
Chen Heng spends accumulated simulation points to purchase starting talents, backgrounds, and cultivation bases before projecting his soul into new worlds. When the simulated life ends, his accumulated comprehension, power, and bloodlines are converted directly back into his main body.

### Is the translation quality good?
The official translation is generally readable and consistent in the early to mid-sections. Later chapters and sci-fi arcs suffer from occasional machine-translation (MTL) artifacts, such as swapped pronouns ("he/she") and abrupt scene transitions.

### Is Fantasy Simulator spicy, gory, or dark?
There is zero explicit sexual content. However, it features moderate to high levels of violence, body horror (such as dissecting magical beasts and devouring divine corpses), and a cold, ruthless protagonist who will sacrifice allies without hesitation.

### Does it end on a cliffhanger?
The main multiversal conflict resolves as Chen Heng reaches peak godhood, but the ending feels open-ended and abrupt as he steps into the Boundary Sea to begin a new journey, leaving some plotlines unresolved.
