Managing a sect is one thing, but guiding an entire immortal bloodline from behind the screen is a whole different beast. *Family Management Game In Immortal Continent* turns clan cultivation into a high-stakes strategy game where every sacrifice from descendants rewards the ancient ancestor.

If you enjoy kingdom-building, multi-generational progression, and watching a small mortal family rise into a dominant superpower, this simulator novel hits all the right notes.

<!--more-->

## Original Description

Li Wei accidentally obtained a simulator that allowed him to manage a family of cultivators. He discovered that the descendants in the game would offer him strange and unique sacrifices.

"Thanks to the ancestor for the gift, our family has become the most powerful family!"

"Hurry up and offer a sacrifice to the ancestor. Please accept this spiritual weapon!"

"Ancestor, we have already conquered the immortal world!"

Then, one day, the descendants of the Li family, who had been in the game, appeared on Earth in grand flying boats. Under the astonished eyes of the entire world, these descendants paid homage to Li Wei in great numbers.

"Welcome our ancestor home!"

## What is in this novel?

The mc has cheat/thing that lets him manage a family within a game. Mc does gets into any life-threatening situation. Low-key and cautious MC. There are mechas!

## FAQ

### Is this novel worth reading?

Up to chapter 55? Yes. After that it depends on how good you are at tracking names. If you're bad at it like me, maybe keep a notes app open.

### Does the MC fight a lot?

Not really. He's low-key and cautious. Don't go in expecting action every chapter.

### Is the mecha tag a big deal?

I thought it would bother me too. It didn't, at least early on. Give it a shot even if you skip mecha stuff normally.

### Why did you drop it at chapter 228?

Wasn't a quality issue — the story is actually good. Just too many characters with similar names and I kept losing track of who's talking. Got tiring.

### Should I still try it?

Read to chapter 20 like the description says. If you're hooked, go. Just mentally prepare for a big cast later.
