## Some Thoughts

I started this novel on 26/06/2025 and read up to chapter 214. The novel is complete in raw (6 Volumes / 3,613 Chapters), but has no full English translation, and it has an ongoing manhua adaptation. I am dropping the novel as it is pure wish-fulfillment with no real consequences, and the MC gets progressively dumber.

## FAQ

#### Q: Is Your Talent is Mine worth reading?

A: Only if you want a complete turn-your-brain-off wish-fulfillment read where the MC gets everything he wants without any struggle. If you care about real stakes, good romance, or consistent power scaling, skip it.

#### Q: How does the cheat ability work?

A: The MC can see everyone's innate talents (sword mastery, cultivation speed, special elements, spatial abilities, etc.) and copy them. He stacks and fuses them freely, becoming overpowered instantly with basically no downsides or limits.

#### Q: How is the romance?

A: Awful. Even the translator left notes making fun of the author's romance writing. Once the female lead shows up around chapter 190, the MC stops caring about getting stronger to survive and his IQ takes a noticeable nosedive.

#### Q: How is the translation quality?

A: Pretty rough. They keep swapping cultivation realm and ranking names around, and the status windows sometimes show mismatched stats and abilities compared to earlier chapters.

#### Q: Why did you drop it at chapter 214?

A: It became pure wish-fulfillment with zero consequences. The power scaling moved so fast that previous realm names didn't even matter, and the cringey romance made the MC act like an idiot.
