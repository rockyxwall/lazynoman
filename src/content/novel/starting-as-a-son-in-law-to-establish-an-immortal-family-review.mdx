## Is Starting As A Son-In-Law Worth It?

**Starting As A Son-In-Law To Establish An Immortal Family is a must-read if you enjoy low-key, overpowered protagonists who build empires through legacy rather than just constant fighting.** The "Many Children, Many Blessings" system provides a unique progression hook that keeps the early and mid-game addictive. Seeing the Lu family rise from a small branch to an eternal immortal dynasty is genuinely satisfying. However, the narrative loses some steam when it shifts too heavily toward the offspring's individual stories. If you liked *The Patriarch is Taking Concubines Again*, you will love this.

**My score: 8.5/10.**

## What Is Starting As A Son-In-Law?

Lu Changsheng is a transmigrator in a brutal Xianxia world. With poor spiritual roots and no hope of joining a major sect, he faces a bleak future until he activates a unique system: **Many Children, Many Blessings**. The system rewards him with cultivation, talents, and resources for every child he fathers, effectively turning his lineage into his power source.

Instead of fighting for every scrap of spirit stone, Lu Changsheng chooses a "safe" path. He marries into a cultivation family as a son-in-law, stays low-key, hides his true power, and focuses entirely on expanding his family tree. The tone is a mix of carefree harem-building and cautious, strategic cultivation, designed for readers who love "weak-to-strong" tropes but prefer a protagonist who wins through schemes and longevity.

## My Review

I have read **1,497 chapters** of this novel. I started it quite a while ago, and for the first 1,200 chapters, I was completely hooked. It is one of the better "system" novels that actually sticks to its core gimmick for a long time without immediately abandoning it for generic world-hopping.

### The Beauty of Being Low-Key

The "low-key" aspect is handled very well. Unlike many MCs who claim to be low-key but provoke everyone they meet, Lu Changsheng actually tries to stay out of trouble. Seeing him "conquer" women to bring them into his family is satisfying because the rewards feel tangible—you see the family grow and the MC get stronger in the background while others are struggling and dying in secret realms. The power-hiding trope is peak here; there’s a specific joy in watching the MC reveal just enough power to protect his family while the world has no idea he’s the true ancestor standing at the top.

### The Offspring Shift: A Double-Edged Sword

The novel was near-perfect until around chapter 1,300 or 1,400. At this point, the narrative begins to focus heavily on the children and the next generations. While this makes sense for a "family building" novel, it made the pacing feel much slower and, frankly, a bit boring. The MC's personal charm is what carries the story, so when he takes a backseat to his numerous offspring, the excitement dips. I liked how the MC conquers the women and gets them to have a child with him, but the shift in focus felt like a distraction from the core appeal.

## Who Is This For?

### Read If…

- You love **system-based progression** that feels rewarding.
- You enjoy **low-key/cautious MCs** who are secretly OP.
- You like **family building** and seeing a small clan become a superpower.
- You are a fan of **harem and romance** being tied to the plot.

### Skip If…

- You want an MC who is always in the middle of every war and battle.
- You dislike "breeding" or "offspring" based power systems.
- You need the narrative to stay 100% focused on the MC at all times.

## What Others Think

The community generally ranks this as one of the best in the "Family Building" sub-genre. Many compare it to *The Patriarch is Taking Concubines Again*, but note that *Son-In-Law* has a slightly more "careful" protagonist. On platforms like Webnovel, it maintains a very high rating because it delivers exactly what it promises: a high-quality power trip.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Starting As A Son-In-Law worth reading in 2026?

Yes. Despite the late-game pacing issues, the first 1,200+ chapters are some of the most enjoyable "clan building" content available in the Xianxia genre. It’s perfect for binge-reading.

### How long is the novel? Is it finished?

As of my last check, the novel has over **1,500 chapters** and is still **ongoing**. It has millions of views on platforms like Webnovel, indicating its continued popularity.

### Does the MC ever stop being low-key?

He stays low-key for as long as possible, which is the draw of the novel. He only reveals his hand when the survival of the Lu family is at stake, which makes those moments much more impactful.
