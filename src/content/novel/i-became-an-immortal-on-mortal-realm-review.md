## What to expect
MC gets reincarnated into cultivation world and has a cheat. Overpowered, never lost a battle. Never made a close call in a battle. Smart and humble. Not low-key. There are some one-sided late romances but no conclusion, as the MC does not have time for it. End of the novel but remained a virgin. It has 485 chapters, completed and fully translated.

## Rreview
This is a Xianxia and Xuanhuan, as you can guess from the title. The initial chapters are exciting because of the MC's chat, then it becomes boring as it's the same old things. The MC has a helper that helps him from the shadows so the MC does not encounter many traumas; everything goes smoothly. Let's skip to the end of the novel. The MC creates his own path at the very end after cultivating the standard way and spreads it on a very small scale, which is why I felt it was a bit similar to [Comprehension Ability: Creating and teaching the Dao in various worlds]. Both MCs comprehend and create their own skills or techniques and carve their own path, and both end the novel as virgins. Lastly, at the end, the MC kills the big enemy — the end.

## Thoughts while reading

Chapter 227, It's getting interesting now that gods are being mentioned.

Chapter 463, Mortal to immortal in days? How? No logic.

Chapter 485, There was no proper conclusion; after the big fight there was just "the end of the book."

## FAQ

#### Does the MC have a harem?
No. There are a few one-sided romances that show up late into the story, but none of them go anywhere. The MC ends the novel as a virgin.

#### Is the MC overpowered from the start?
Yes. He reincarnates with a cheat, never loses a battle, and never really gets pushed into a corner. That's just how it is here.

#### Does the MC follow a standard cultivation path?
For most of the novel, yes — but at the very end he breaks off from it entirely and creates his own cultivation path, which he spreads on a small scale.

#### Does the story have a proper ending?
Not really. After the final fight it just stops. No epilogue, no wrap-up.

#### Is alchemy or crafting involved?
Not from the MC's side. He almost exclusively develops attack-related skills, which gets a bit repetitive after a while.

#### Do you need to read any prior novels to understand this one?
No. It's standalone.
