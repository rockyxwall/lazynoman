## The Short Version

Funny, shameless MC. Great world. Weak ending. Perfect starter novel — don't expect depth.

---

## My Review
This was my first Chinese cultivation novel. I went in blind, had zero comparisons, and genuinely loved it. Take my 10/10 with that context.

Lin Fan is loud, shameless, prideful, and hilarious. His entire skill set is built around things that should not exist in a non-adult novel — somehow the author kept it funny instead of creepy. He has actual morals and integrity though, he is not brain-dead, which puts him above a lot of OP MCs in this genre.

The weak-to-overpowered arc works well. World building is solid. The comedy carries the whole thing.

The ending is bad. Not "disappointing" bad — actually bad. It felt like the author got tired and just stopped. Left a real bad taste after investing that much time.

## Read or Skip?

**Read if —** you want something light and funny, you're new to the genre, you enjoy shameless & overpowered MC, low stakes is fine with you.

**Skip if —** you need serious plot, consistent logic in the power system, meaningful side characters, or a good ending.

---

## What Others Think

Almost everyone agrees: the first 400 chapters are genuinely fun, the comedy works, and Lin Fan's energy is refreshing. After that it gets repetitive — same jokes, same patterns, no tension since the MC literally cannot lose. The system has no real rules so anything can happen at any time, which kills stakes completely.

The ending is the one thing nearly every reader agrees on — rushed, strange, and unsatisfying. Multiple readers who finished it described it as setup for the author's other novels rather than a real conclusion.

Casual readers and genre newcomers enjoy it. Veterans find it hollow past the halfway point.

**Fun start, weak finish. Read early.**
